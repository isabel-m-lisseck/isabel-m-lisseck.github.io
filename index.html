Digital Sovereignty Benchmark Assessment

IBM Institute for Business Value · 2026

This survey is designed for digital sovereignty decision makers and takes less than 5 minutes to complete.

Your responses will generate a personalised benchmark report showing how your organisation compares to peers across four key dimensions of digital sovereignty. Click each dimension to learn more:

  • Data Sovereignty ›
    The right to control where your data is stored, how it is processed, who can access it, and which legal jurisdiction governs it. This includes encryption key ownership, cross-border data flows, and enforcement of governance policies across all environments and vendors.
  • Operational Sovereignty ›
    The ability of your organisation to continue operating critical systems independently of external services, even during disruptions, outages, or third-party restrictions. It also covers your visibility and control over all critical technology and service dependencies.
  • Tech Sovereignty ›
    Your organisation's ability to deploy and manage compute, storage, and network resources independently, run critical software without vendor lock-in, and move systems and data between platforms or vendors with minimal constraint. Built on open standards and portability by design.
  • AI Sovereignty ›
    Control over AI model development, training data, deployment, and outputs — free from dependence on external entities. This includes ownership of model decisions, explainability, auditability, and alignment with the EU AI Act and internal governance policies.
